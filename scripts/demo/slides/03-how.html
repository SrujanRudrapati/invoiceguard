How it works

The model reads

Haiku extracts each field with a verbatim quote from the page.

Sonnet only when Haiku’s reading can’t be verified.

Software checks evidence and math

Every quote must exist on the page and match its value.

Line items, subtotal, tax, total and dates are checked in code.

People decide the exceptions

A keyboard-driven review desk shows each reason and its evidence.

Every correction becomes a new eval case.
